Another milestone reached! I've now been muddling through life for forty years! Where does the time go!?!
It's been a long while now since I've blogged, but life has got in the way. Not a bad thing if you ask me!

Since my last update, there have been several dramatic changes in the world. COVID-19 hit the world a few
months after my last post and work became a very different animal. I started working from home the week 
before the first lockdown in the UK which started on 23rd March 2020, and since then I have remained doing 
the majority of my work from home. I started back on site on a regular basis is September 2020, but it was 
a huge change from pre-COVID times. Some projects I have visited weekly, some fortnightly and some monthly,
but there have been several projects since then that I have only assisted with procurement, pre-construction
or claims so have not been anywhere near the site.








As I did when I was [30][30], I feel I should probably look back at my [Epic Quest][eq] that I started back
in 2013. I've managed to pick up on a few bits over the last 10 years, but not a huge amount, so here is a 
full update of what I've achieved on the quest in the last decade...

### Completed Quests
